# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free project space for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, constructive and respectful. Focus feedback on the work, assume good intent and respect differing perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates these standards. Report concerns privately to the project owner through the repository's configured contact channel.
